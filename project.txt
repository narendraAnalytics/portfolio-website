VAAKYA — Vaakya is an autonomous, multi-agent AI platform that eliminates the legal document bottleneck for Indian small and medium businesses (SMBs). Powered by LangGraph, FastAPI, and Claude AI, Vaakya handles the entire legal document lifecycle - from a user typing a plain-English request or uploading a PDF

desc : Vaakya transforms legal document management with intelligent AI agents that automate drafting, contract review, redlining, risk detection, and obligation tracking. Built for businesses, it simplifies complex legal workflows into a fast, secure, and efficient experience
